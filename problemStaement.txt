Problem Statement: AML Alert Triage Copilot

================================================================================
CONTEXT
================================================================================
Banks run transaction monitoring systems that flag suspicious activity, such as 
cash deposits kept just under reporting limits (structuring), money that passes 
straight through an account (layering/pass-through), or activity far beyond a 
customer’s declared income. Each flag becomes an alert that an Anti-Money 
Laundering (AML) analyst must review before deciding to close it, investigate 
further, or escalate it as a suspicious transaction report (STR).

================================================================================
THE PROBLEM
================================================================================
Monitoring rules are deliberately broad, because missing a real money laundering 
case carries severe regulatory penalties. The result is that most alerts turn out 
to be false positives (frequently >90%). For each alert, analysts manually gather 
the customer’s KYC profile, transaction history, and counterparties from several 
disparate systems before they can make an informed judgment.

This creates three critical problems:
1. Slow triage: Analysts spend most of their time collecting context, not making 
   judgments.
2. Alert fatigue: High volumes of false positives reduce attention on genuinely 
   suspicious cases.
3. Inconsistent decisions and documentation: Different analysts reach different 
   conclusions on similar alerts, and the reasoning is not always recorded well 
   enough for audit and regulatory scrutiny.

================================================================================
USERS
================================================================================
- Primary: AML analysts who triage alerts daily.
- Secondary: Compliance managers, Quality Assurance (QA) teams, and regulatory 
  auditors who review decisions and STR narrative quality.

================================================================================
GOAL
================================================================================
Reduce the time an analyst spends per alert and improve decision consistency, 
without reducing the detection of real suspicious activity.

================================================================================
PROPOSED SOLUTION
================================================================================
A GenAI copilot that, for each alert:
1. Summarises why the alert fired and what is unusual compared with the 
   customer’s profile.
2. Suggests a disposition (Close, Investigate, or Escalate) with clear reasons, 
   each explicitly citing its source data.
3. Says "insufficient evidence" instead of guessing or hallucinating when critical 
   data is absent.
4. Drafts an STR narrative for escalations, clearly marked as a draft.
5. Records every AI suggestion, reasoning diff, and analyst decision in an 
   immutable audit log.

================================================================================
DESIGN PRINCIPLE
================================================================================
The AI prepares and recommends. The analyst decides. 
The copilot NEVER closes an alert or files a report autonomously.

================================================================================
SUCCESS METRICS
================================================================================
- Primary Metric: Average time spent per alert.
- Guardrail Metric: Missed escalations on the evaluation set must stay at ZERO (0% false negatives).
- Alignment Metric: Agreement rate between AI suggestions and senior analyst decisions.
- Efficiency Metric: Share of false positives correctly suggested for closure.
- Governance Metric: Audit completeness (100% of decisions have recorded, auditable rationales).

================================================================================
OUT OF SCOPE (PROTOTYPE)
================================================================================
- Real customer or bank data (synthetic/fictional data only).
- Building underlying detection rules or machine learning scoring models.
- Autonomous alert closure or automatic STR filing.
- Production IAM/SSO login, multi-tenant roles, and direct integrations with core banking/monitoring systems.

================================================================================
PROTOTYPE NOTE
================================================================================
Built with fictional data to demonstrate product judgment, safety guardrails, 
and rigorous evaluation design for generative AI in financial crime compliance.
