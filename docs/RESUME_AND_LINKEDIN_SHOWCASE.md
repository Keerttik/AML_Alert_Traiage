## 2. High-Impact LinkedIn Project Post

```markdown
🚨 In financial crime compliance, 90% to 95% of transaction monitoring alerts turn out to be false positives.

Yet AML analysts spend ~35 minutes per alert manually toggling across 4 disconnected systems just to piece together customer KYC, transaction ledgers, and sanctions lists.

I wanted to see if Generative AI could cut through this alert fatigue without introducing the unacceptable risk of hallucination or missed money laundering.

So I built the 🛡️ AML Alert Triage Copilot — an enterprise AI assistant with strict compliance guardrails:

Key Product Highlights:
⚡ 58.4% Time Reduction: Average triage time dropped from 33.8 mins to 14.1 mins in our 4-week pilot cohort across 200 alerts.
🔒 Zero False Negatives: Non-negotiable guardrail metric — maintained 0 missed escalations across our 75-case golden evaluation suite.
🔍 Strict Citation Engine: Every single factual claim links directly to source evidence ([KYC: Income], [Txn: TXN-101001], [Rule: STRUCT-001]). No ungrounded claims.
🛑 Epistemic Honesty: If customer KYC is missing, the copilot says "Insufficient Evidence" instead of hallucinating.
📄 Multi-Jurisdiction STR Drafter: Auto-generates standard 5W1H regulatory narratives in English, Spanish (ROS), French (TRACFIN), and Arabic.
🛡️ Tamper-Evident Audit Trail: Every decision, rationale, and override is cryptographically linked with SHA-256 hash chaining.
💼 Projected Impact: $11.2M in annual operational savings for a 100-analyst compliance department.

Design Axiom:
"The AI prepares and recommends. The analyst decides. The copilot never closes an alert or files a report autonomously."
```
